## 🧩 Problem Description
Given a fence with `n` posts and `k` colors, find the number of ways to paint the fence such that **no more than two consecutive posts** have the same color.

## 📌 Examples

**Example 1:**
> **Input:** `n = 3, k = 2`
> **Output:** `6`
> **Explanation:** Colors {R, B}. Valid combinations: RRB, RBR, RBB, BRR, BRB, BBR.

**Example 2:**
> **Input:** `n = 2, k = 4`
> **Output:** `16`
> **Explanation:** All combinations of 2 colors are valid. $4 \times 4 = 16$.

---

## 📐 Constraints
- $1 \le n \le 500$
- $1 \le k \le 10^5$
- Result fits in a 64-bit integer (typically, though some platforms say 32).
